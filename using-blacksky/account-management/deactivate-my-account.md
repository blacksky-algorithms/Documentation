# Deactivate my account

If you want to deactivate your account, you can do so from within your account settings.&#x20;

1. Visit[ blacksky.community](https://blacksky.community/)
2. Log in to your account
3. Navigate to Settings
4. Select Account
5. Click D

If you’d like to [reactivate your account](reactivate-my-account.md), it’s as simple as logging back in.
